The midGard Staff

C4 is Middle C on the piano.

The staff expands vertically as needed. Each four-line group represents F–G–A–H.

The standard layout has six groups: two pink, two blue, and two pink. Additional groups are green.

The two central blue groups form the permanent core and must always remain. The surrounding groups may be added or removed according to the music.
