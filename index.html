midGard Sheet Music

A TypeScript & SVG Sheet Music Project

An experimental sheet music editor and reader, built with TypeScript and SVG. Our goal is to create beautiful, precise musical notation for piano, with future support for MIDI import, editing, and the midGard notation system.
